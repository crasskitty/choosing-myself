## Description

Choosing Myself is a private counter for the days after you stop texting them.

It keeps the count. It gives you one quiet line each day. When the urge hits, it walks you through a minute of breathing, then gives you a page for the message you do not send. Lock it away. The streak stays.

Nothing in here is an account. There is no feed, no cloud, and no one else reading what you write. The letters stay on this phone.

What you can do:

- See the day count from the morning no contact started. The count is always free.
- Read today's line. One a day, from a bundled set you can edit if you build the app yourself.
- Ride out the urge: a minute of breathing, then an unsent letter.
- Look back at the letters you locked away.
- Mark 7, 30, 60, 90, and 365 days.
- Set a daily reminder for that day's line.
- If you slip, start today as day one. Letters stay either way.

A one-time unlock opens the rest of the room: every mood (Rain, Night Drive, Ocean), letters without the limit of three, a Face ID or passcode lock, shareable story cards, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and keeping your best streak if you slip. No subscription.

This is a companion to the choosing.myself page. It is not therapy, not medical care, and not a crisis service. If you need more than a quiet counter, use local help.

## App Review notes

No account. No password. Open the app, pick a start date, and the day count works without a purchase.

The unlock is a non-consumable, product id `com.choosingmyself.unlock`. Please use a sandbox Apple ID to buy it. Restore is on the unlock screen and in Settings.

The day counter and today's line are free. The purchase adds moods beyond the one chosen at the start, letters past three, Face ID, milestone image cards, widgets, and keeping a best streak after a reset.

Face ID is optional and off until it is turned on in Settings. The usage string explains that it keeps unsent letters private.

Local notifications are optional. The widget uses the App Group and shows the day count and today's line only after the unlock. Letters are not in the widget or the notification.

This app is not a medical device.
