## What this app is

Choosing Myself is a private no-contact counter. It is not an account, a social network, or a medical service.

## Data on your phone

The app stores, on the device only:

- the date you said no contact started
- an optional nickname for the other person
- the mood you picked
- your reminder time, if you turn reminders on
- whether the lock is on
- your day count and best streak
- the unsent letters you choose to lock away

That information stays in the app's storage on the phone. The widget reads only the day count, the mood, today's line, and whether the unlock is active. Letters are not written to the widget.

If you back this phone up (iCloud or a computer backup), Apple's backup can include the app's data. That backup is controlled by your device settings, not by a server we operate. This app has no server.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect:

- an account, email, or phone number
- contacts, photos, location, or browsing
- analytics, advertising identifiers, or a tracking profile
- the text of your letters

There is no third-party analytics or advertising SDK in the app.

## Purchases

The optional unlock is a one-time purchase through Apple. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your card number. Apple tells the app whether this Apple ID has unlocked it. Restoring a purchase uses Apple's purchase history.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS schedules it on the phone. The notification is today's line and the day count. It can appear on the Lock Screen. Letters are not notified.

## Face ID

If you turn on the lock, the phone uses Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode to open the app. The app does not receive or store your biometric data. That stays with the system.

## The help link

Settings includes a link to the International Association for Suicide Prevention's local-help page (https://www.iasp.info/suicidalthoughts/). That opens in the browser only if you tap it. We do not learn that you tapped it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
